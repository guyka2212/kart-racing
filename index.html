Feels like a real kart

Grip that builds as your tyres warm up, karts with real weight, and a launch that rewards a clean start.

Made for a wheel

Calibrate any HID wheel (Logitech, Thrustmaster, Fanatec, Moza) in a few steps: steering range, pedals and your buttons. Set rotation, steering lock and deadzones to taste.

Race in VR

Sit in the kart with an OpenXR headset (Quest Link, SteamVR). You see out of a helmet, the menus float in front of you, and a screen on the steering wheel shows speed, position and tyres.

Tyres that matter

Cold tyres slide, warm tyres bite, worn tyres fade. Watch all four on the HUD or the wheel screen, and dive into the pits for fresh rubber.

Pit lane & crew

Start from the grid or the pit lane. The speed limiter kicks in, the crew does the tyre change, and you rejoin the race.

Bots that race clean

Opponents hold their line, brake for slower karts and pick a gap to pass instead of punting you off. Pick one level or a mixed field.

Your buttons

Button mapping for keyboard & mouse, controller and wheel, with two buttons per action. A hold-to-look-back button shows who's behind you.

Get on track

Free. No account, no ads.

Download KartRacingSetup.exe All versions
  1. Run the installer. It installs for your user account, so no admin password is needed. Pick a Start menu or desktop shortcut.
  2. Plug in your wheel or controller (optional). With a wheel, open Settings › Calibrate wheel & pedals once.
  3. Race. Pick a track, set the laps and opponents, and start from the grid or the pit lane.
Windows SmartScreen: the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say it "protected your PC". Click More info › Run anyway. Uninstall any time from Windows Settings › Apps.
